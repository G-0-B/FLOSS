## Core loop
Recursive four-stage loop: Reframe → Prime → Do → Sustain

### 1. Reframe (Compass)
Function: expand measurement space. Convert threat frame to possibility topology.
Exit criteria: at least one gain hypothesis with falsifier logged.

### 2. Prime (Backpack)
Function: lock uncertainty balancers before exposure.
Minimum balancers: 1 identity anchor, 1 relationship anchor, 1 resource anchor.
Exit criteria: balancer registry committed, rollback conditions defined.

### 3. Do (Sailboat)
Function: execute smallest reversible probe toward adjacent possible.
Constraint: undo cost < 5 percent of resource or timebox < 48 hours. Do not force machinery when readiness signals absent.
Exit criteria: probe executed or aborted, outcome logged.

### 4. Sustain (Anchor)
Function: consolidate learning and repair setpoints.
Exit criteria: internal progress logged separately from external outcome, next loop seeded.

## Key primitives
- Transilience: log abrupt light-cone expansion events with pre/post state vectors.
- Don't Force Machinery: wait condition; if balancer health < threshold, hold in Prime.
- Frontier operation: allocate 5-15 percent attention to boundary nodes.
- Infinite game lens: keep continuation criterion visible in every Reframe.
